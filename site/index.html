LazyScroll

Look down. It scrolls.

LazyScroll is a Chrome extension for hands-free browsing. Look at the bottom of any page and it scrolls down; look at the top and it scrolls up. On X, smile at a post to like it.

Face tracking runs entirely on your computer. Nothing is uploaded, and there's no account or API key.
