# LazyScroll privacy policy

_Last updated: 12 October 2026_

LazyScroll uses your webcam to estimate where you are looking and whether you are smiling, so it can scroll the
page and like posts on x.com for you.

**What it processes.** Video frames from your webcam, only while an x.com tab is in front or the LazyScroll setup
page is open. Each frame is analysed on your device by a face-tracking model bundled inside the extension and then
discarded immediately.

**What it stores.** Only on your device, in the browser's extension storage: your settings and your calibration
(a few dozen numbers describing how your eye position maps to your screen, plus your smile sensitivity). No images
or video are ever stored.

**What it sends.** Nothing. LazyScroll makes no network requests. It has no servers, no analytics, no ads and no
accounts. Your camera feed, calibration and settings never leave your computer.

**What it does on x.com.** It reads the page to find the post in the middle of your screen, scrolls the page, and
clicks the like button when you smile. It does not read your messages, collect posts, or access your X account in
any other way.

**Removing your data.** Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored. You can also revoke camera access
at any time in Chrome's site settings for the extension.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Raimo33/LazyScroll.
