2 Calibrate

About 20 seconds, full screen. Sit the way you normally browse. Follow the dot with your eyes (9 spots across the screen), then glance at your keyboard, then smile slightly. This teaches LazyScroll how your eyes map to your screen, where "below the screen" starts, and how big your smile is. Redo it if you move your laptop, camera or chair a lot.

3 Settings

Changes apply instantly. The toolbar button turns LazyScroll on and off, everywhere or just for the current site. It only tracks while the browser window is focused, and pauses while you type.
